# New Pokémon Snap

![New Pokémon Snap companion status: coverage, how current it is, and spoiler control](assets/readme-status-card.svg)

A free New Pokémon Snap hint guide with spoiler levels you control. It covers Nintendo Switch version 2.0.0, including the free update courses. Your AI agent answers only from the guide files, at the level you set.

## Use it

Install the [reader](https://github.com/hintforge/reader) in an AI agent that can read files on your computer, then tell it:

> Load the New Pokémon Snap guide from github.com/hintforge/pokemon-snap

Try "how do I raise a course's research level" or "give me a small nudge on this request".

The guide uses the [Hintforge](https://github.com/hintforge/builder) format.

## Spoilers

Two independent dials control surprise-encounter warnings (Tier 0 to 5) and puzzle warnings (Tier 0 to 3). Both default to Tier 0: no unsolicited encounter or request information. Ask for a nudge, more help or a full walkthrough of a request. Puzzle Tier 3 gives full solutions automatically.

## What's inside

- All 234 **LenTalk requests**, grouped by course in [`puzzles/`](puzzles/index.md). Entries give research levels, posting triggers where sourced, requester, reward, photo requirements, solutions and sources.
- Unlocks for all 30 courses, research points per level and alternate routes in [`puzzles/index.md`](puzzles/index.md). The points counter resets after each level-up.
- Photo scoring, research levels, NEO-ONE tools, course structure, controls and settings.

Request pages draw on Grindosaur, Serebii and Samurai-Gamers, with sources linked per entry. Per-species behavior pages are still thin.
