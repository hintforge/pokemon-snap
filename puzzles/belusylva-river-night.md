## Course: Mightywide River: River (Night)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Mightywide River's nighttime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Mightywide River's daytime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 50,000, MAX = 90,000
- **Alternate routes:** Mightywide River (Night) has one alternate route. Pass the large waterfall containing Gyrados. Scan the overgrown wall where three Beautifly rest in vines to select the short cave route.

## Requests (3)

### Diving Spot

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 and above of this course.
- **From:** Todd · **Reward:** Black-and-White (Filter)
- **Must be in the photo:** Feraligatr
- **Request text:** "Phil said the NEO-ONE suddenly shifted course around here, and he also heard a loud noise. I wonder if something fell!"
- **Solution:**
  - Hit Feraligatr on the cliff with Fluffruit. Photograph its dive as it enters the water; Burst Mode may help.
- *(sources: https://www.serebii.net/newpokemonsnap/missions/divingspot.shtml ; https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/requests/diving-spot · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*

### Majestic Ursaring

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Phil · **Reward:** Star 6 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Magikarp, Ursaring
- **Request text:** "I got this shot of Ursaring hunting for Magikarp! I know I was just moments away from getting a great shot!"
- **Solution:**
  - In Level 2, get to the waterfall where there's a Crystabloom. Hit the Crystabloom and use Scan and the Magikarp will jump and and slap the Ursaring in the face for a photo
- *(sources: https://www.serebii.net/newpokemonsnap/missions/majesticursaring.shtml ; https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/requests/majestic-ursaring · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*

### Lurking in the Water

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** Effect 7 (Frame)
- **Must be in the photo:** Gyarados
- **Request text:** "See that gash in that rocky cliff? I'm guessing an awfully large Pokémon must have had something to do with it! Could you look into it for me?"
- **Solution:**
  - On level 2, wake both sleeping Magikarp beside the river with melody, then Illumina Orbs. They swim to the waterfall. Orb the Crystabloom behind it so the Magikarp attempt to jump up. Photograph Gyarados when it appears and climbs the waterfall.
- *(sources: https://www.serebii.net/newpokemonsnap/missions/lurkinginthewater.shtml ; https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/requests/lurking-in-the-water · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*
