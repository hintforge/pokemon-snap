## Course: Outaway Cave: Cave

- **How the course unlocks:** The Outaway Cave's cave system unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Shiver Snowfields' nighttime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 35,000, Lv. 3 = 55,000, MAX = 70,000
- **Alternate routes:** Outaway Cave has three alternate paths. Level 1 follows the standard route; level 2 follows a different route, selectable at the Croagunk river on level 3. For the second route, enter the Giant Crystals chamber on level 2 or 3. Orb Mawile and both Carbink to summon Diancie, then orb Diancie. Its song calms Mawile ahead, opening another lush undergrowth area. For the third route, stay on the main course toward Rimstone cave. Past the Croagunk river, orb Crobat hanging on the left ceiling. Play melody or scan so Gengar scares the next Crobat. At the tunnel's end, enter the large chamber and select the route closer to the Rimstone pools.
