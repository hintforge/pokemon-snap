## Course: Elsewhere Forest: Forest

- **How the course unlocks:** The forest portion of the Elsewhere Forest unlocks after reaching the Fireflow Volcano's second research level.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 40,000, Lv. 3 = 70,000, MAX = 80,000
- **Alternate routes:** Elsewhere Forest has two alternate routes and four seasonal biomes. On level 2+, play melody beside the floating boulder on the right near the start. Trevenant moves; scan to enter a route reaching two forest areas. After a seasonal biome, return to the fog and complete the Deerling event: light the first two Crystablooms, only the rear flower of the next pair, then the next flower. Espeon, Deerling and Sawsbuck gather. At the next prompt, scan and select the route to unlock it permanently. Biome choice is random unless you influence Espeon; hitting it with Fluffruit also leaves the choice random.
