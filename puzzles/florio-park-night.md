## Course: Florio Nature Park: Park (Night)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Florio Nature Park's nighttime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Florio Nature Park's daytime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 50,000, MAX = 110,000
- **Alternate routes:** Florio Nature Park (Night) has the same two alternate routes as daytime. Bidoof's dam opens at research level 2 rather than 3. After the story cutscene introducing the NEO-ONE's shrinking device, Secret Side Path opens on any level. Follow the main route to Swanna's pond, then scan the lone tree across the path before the dam and select the route.
