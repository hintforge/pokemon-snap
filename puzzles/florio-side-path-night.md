## Requests (1)

### The Parachute

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 of this course.
- **From:** Todd · **Reward:** Poké Ball (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Foongus
- **Request text:** "I bet getting a shot of Foongus when they're floating down from the air would be worth the effort! Why don't you give it a try?"
- **Solution:**
  - When you first bump down on the Foongus, if you look down there's a group of Foongus. Throw an Illumina Orb at them and they will jump up and float down
  - On Secret Side Path (Night) level 1, skip the early alternate route and descend into the valley. Look down and right for Foongus in a crack. Orb any one to make them jump. Photograph them descending into the crack on their mushroom caps, scattering pollen that lights the surroundings.
