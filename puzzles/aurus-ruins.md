## Course: Ruins of Remembrance: Ruins

- **How the course unlocks:** The ancient ruins portion of the Ruins of Remembrance becomes accessible by completing the Outaway Cave's Illumina Spot at least once.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 40,000, Lv. 3 = 50,000, MAX = 40,000
- **Alternate routes:** At the end of Ruins of Remembrance, light six underground Crystablooms to activate the altar route and unlock the final story research course. Find them beside the blue Meganium statue; beside the red Wishiwashi statue; behind stalagmites past the purple Steelix statue; behind Eldegoss near the green Volcarona statue; then behind Golurk, which must move. Finally, orb the unlit flower in the Crystabloom circle.

### Just Perching Here

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 of this course.
- **From:** Rita · **Reward:** Rita's Hairpin (Profile Image)
- **Must be in the photo:** Natu
- **Request text:** "I threw a fluffruit earlier, and a Natu came down and perched on it! It was so adorable!"
- **Solution:**
  - In Level 1, get a picture of Natu perching on Fluffruit by luring it out of the ruins with the Fluffruit
  - On Ruins of Remembrance level 1, follow the narrow passage to the ruins. Above the targeting point is a small rubble opening. Toss Fluffruit into or against it, even if it bounces back. Photograph Natu emerging, jumping down and perching on the fruit, then finish the course.
