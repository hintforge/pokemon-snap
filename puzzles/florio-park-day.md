## Course: Florio Nature Park: Park (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Florio Nature Park's daytime course is available from the start.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 40,000, Lv. 3 = 70,000, MAX = 90,000
- **Alternate routes:** Florio Nature Park (Day) has two alternate routes. The route over Bidoof's dam opens at research level 3. For Secret Side Path, first reach the story cutscene introducing the NEO-ONE's shrinking device. On any level, follow the main route to Swanna's pond. Before the dam, scan the lone tree across the path and select Secret Side Path for a different photo angle.

### Shockingly Well-Done

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 and above of this course. Posts when: Unlocked after clearing the first Illumina Spot.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** Effect 4 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Emolga
- **Request text:** "Have you seen which Pokémon is scorching these fruits? I'd like you to try to catch the culprit in the act, if you wouldn't mind."
- **Solution:**
  - Get a picture of Emolga using its electricity on a fruit by throwing a Fluffruit towards the scorched fruit
  - Step by step: (1) Past the first stream, find burnt Fluffruits beside a tree on the right; scan to examine them. → (2) Place Fluffruit nearby to attract Emolga. → (3) It zaps the fresh fruit. → (4) Photograph it before it finishes.

### One-Hand Freeze

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 3 of this course. Posts when: Unlocked after clearing the first request.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** Professor Mirror's Goggles (Profile Image)
- **Must be in the photo:** Grookey
- **Request text:** "Watch Grookey in the nature park carefully. Every now and then, it does something that I think you'll get a kick out of."
- **Solution:**
  - Get a picture of Grookey in the flowerfield doing a handstand on one arm. This is done by waking it up in the middle area. It will do its handstand to show off for you either there or in the flower fields and playing Melody
  - On Florio Nature Park (Day) level 3, wake Grookey in the tall grass near Swanna's pond by hitting it with several Fluffruit. It eats, searches nearby, then does a one-armed handstand. Wake it early and photograph the handstand before you travel too far. The flower field offers a second, easier chance.
  - In Florio Nature Park's flower field, feed Grookey Fluffruit. Wait briefly, then photograph its one-armed handstand. This spot offers an easier shot.
  - Step by step: (1) Find the tall bushes beside Florio Nature Park (Day)'s lake. → (2) Wake Grookey with Fluffruit and wait for it to eat. → (3) Follow it and photograph its one-armed handstand.
