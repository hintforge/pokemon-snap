## Course: Shiver Snowfields: Snowfields (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Shiver Snowfields' daytime course unlocks after completing the Lental Seafloors' Illumina Spot at least once.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 25,000, Lv. 3 = 65,000, MAX = 110,000
- **Alternate routes:** Shiver Snowfields (Day) has two alternate routes. Near the start, photograph Alolan Sandslash atop the large snow pile on the left. It burrows away, then reappears on another left-hand pile. Photograph it again. Near the incline to the snowy plateau, wait for it to dig into the snowbank, then scan for the pine ravine route. To unlock the Icy Cave route, use that first route at night. Orb Crabominable on the right in the pine forest, then orb it again near a pine tree. Its punch drops snow onto sleeping Abomasnow. The awakened Abomasnow creates a blizzard revealing Froslass. Photograph Froslass to open the cave route.
