## Course: Mightywide River: River (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Mightywide River's daytime course becomes available after completing the game.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 45,000, Lv. 3 = 55,000, MAX = 60,000
- **Alternate routes:** Mightywide River (Day) has one alternate route, unlocked at night first. Past the large waterfall, scan the right-hand vines holding three Beautifly. Select the route into the short cave system.

## Requests (4)

### Psychic Sidekick

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 or 2 of this course.
- **From:** Phil · **Reward:** Hand Colored (Filter)
- **Must be in the photo:** Magikarp, Psyduck
- **Request text:** "Hey, you gotta hear this! I saw Magikarp floating in the air, but it definitely wasn't using Splash! I wonder what that was all about."
- **Solution:**
  - On level 1, rescue Psyduck from the chasing Aipom. Before the rapids, orb Magikarp out of the river. Psyduck approaches and uses Psychic. Photograph both with Magikarp airborne.

### Cleffa's Training

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 3 of this course.
- **From:** Rita · **Reward:** Crown 3 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Cleffa
- **Request text:** "I can tell they're practicing something, but I think they're having a hard time. Let's keep watching over them and cheer them on until they succeed!"
- **Solution:**
  - On level 3, enter the cave and scan to bring Cleffa off the right ledge. In the main room, repeatedly orb the Crystabloom. Four Cleffa emerge and use Metronome, creating a sand twister. Photograph them with it.
