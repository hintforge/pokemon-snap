## Course: Founja Jungle: Jungle (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Founja Jungle's daytime course becomes available after completing the Florio Nature Park's Illumina Spot at least once.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 35,000, Lv. 3 = 55,000, MAX = 70,000
- **Alternate routes:** Founja Jungle (Day) has two alternate routes. Unlock the first at night: orb the Crystabloom before the tall grass near Quagsire's pond, scan the glowing paw prints and hold X to select the route. It then becomes available by day. The waterfall route has no prerequisites. At the cliffside sloping tree, orb Liepard on the large stone pillar before it lies down. It leaps into the foliage. At the NEO-ONE scan prompt, scan toward the waterfalls and select the route behind them.
