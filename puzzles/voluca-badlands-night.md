## Course: Barren Badlands: Badlands (Night)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Barren Badlands' nighttime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Barren Badlands' daytime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 50,000, MAX = 90,000
- **Alternate routes:** For Barren Badlands (Night)'s alternate route, gather at least three Diglett by the large boulder beside the Crystabloom. Orb a dust cloud, then hit the emerging Diglett with Fluffruit. Repeat three times. Once they gather, orb the Crystabloom so they push the boulder off the ledge. Select the route before passing the geyser.

## Requests (6)

### Guardian of Friends

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Todd · **Reward:** Flower 5 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Mandibuzz, Rockruff
- **Request text:** "Rockruff may be small, but they're brave. They protect their friends in times of need."
- **Solution:**
  - When taking the alternate path, you will see Rockruff and its friends sleeping. Wake the Rockruff up as the Mandibuzz appears and then throw an Illumina Orb at it. It will fight back. Take a picture of the fight

### Crust-side Down

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** Glasses 6 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Crustle
- **Request text:** "That boulder Crustle carries on its back is solid and has quite a bit of weight. I imagine it'd be tough for it to right itself if it's ever flipped on its back."
- **Solution:**
  - Wake Tyranitar up using a Fluffruit from far away so it doesn't see you. It will move towards the nearby Crustle and whack it with its tail causing it to go upside down. Grab a photo of it there
- *(sources: https://www.serebii.net/newpokemonsnap/missions/crust-sidedown.shtml ; https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/requests/crust-side-down · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*

### Gassy Swamp Battle

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Phil · **Reward:** Effect 8 (Frame)
- **Must be in the photo:** Gliscor, Scolipede
- **Request text:** "Heh, I saw something awesome! Even though its opponent was midair, that Scolipede launched an amazing attack."
- **Solution:**
  - Orb Sandshrew on the left to roll it downhill. Wake Diglett in its path so Sandshrew hits it. Photograph them to move them toward the swamp, where Sandshrew wakes Scolipede. Meanwhile, hit Gliscor with Fluffruit so it glides to the Minior craters. After Minior strikes, Gliscor approaches Scolipede and they fight.
- *(sources: https://www.serebii.net/newpokemonsnap/missions/gassyswampbattle.shtml ; https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/requests/gassy-swamp-battle · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*

### Sleepy Gliscor

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Phil · **Reward:** none listed
- **Must be in the photo:** Gliscor
- **Request text:** "I saw a Gliscor yawning! I never see 'em during the day, so I thought that's probably when they sleep... But if that's the case, why'd it look so sleepy?"
- **Solution:**
  - Orb both Vivillon on the left to recruit others, then open the alternate path. Turbo to the Geyser and hit Gliscor with Fluffruit, moving it to the left platform. Orb the trailing Sandstorm Pattern Vivillon to wake those on the wall. Their Sleep Power makes Gliscor yawn. Photograph the yawn.

### Illusion of the Badlands

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** Sparkles 5 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Zeraora
- **Request text:** "I hear that Zeraora sometimes changes something about its appearance. Could you get a picture of that for me?"
- **Solution:**
  - On level 2, wake Zeraora with Fluffruit and let it reach the cliffs. Wake Tyranitar with Fluffruit from far enough away to stay unseen. It makes a sandstorm; Zeraora intervenes and lights itself with electricity. Photograph Zeraora glowing.

### Valley of Vivillon

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** none listed
- **Must be in the photo:** Vivillon
- **Request text:** "We found some Vivillon scales here from a Vivillon form I didn't expect to see in this climate. Can you check it out?"
- **Solution:**
  - Orb the three Vivillon flying away. Quickly orb and photograph at least five Diglett, then hit the Crystabloom to open the alternate path. The area's Vivillon fly toward the valley. Hit the Crystabloom beneath them to bring out more Vivillon. Photograph the group.
