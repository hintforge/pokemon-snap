## Course: Secret Side Path: Side Path (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** To unlock the Secret Side Path's daytime course, progress through New Pokémon Snap's main story until you learn of the NEO-ONE's shrinking ability. Now, visit the Florio Nature Park's daytime course and follow the regular path until you reach Swanna's pond. Here, on the opposite side of the trail, will be a lone tree. Scan it to discover an alternative route leading into the Secret Side Path. Completing this detour will unlock the Secret Side Path's daytime course in your research course's selection menu.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): MAX = 100,000
- **Alternate routes:** Secret Side Path (Day) has one alternate route, regardless of how you enter the course. Near the start, Dodrio chases Pichu and Scorbunny away. Once Dodrio leaves through the left foliage, scan and select the route to Snorlax's hiding place.

## Requests (2)

### Dancing Trio

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 of this course.
- **From:** Rita · **Reward:** none listed
- **Must be in the photo:** Shroomish
- **Request text:** "Phil said he saw three Shroomish dancing together, but I'm pretty sure I've only ever seen one!"
- **Solution:**
  - Get a photo of the Shroomish dancing by lighting up the Crystabloom and then playing the Melody
  - On Secret Side Path (Day) level 1, stay on the regular route and hop onto Emolga to reach the valley bottom. On the right, orb the Crystabloom beside Shroomish to wake them. Play melody and photograph all three Shroomish dancing together.

### Snorlax Dash

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 of this course.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** Hat 7 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Snorlax
- **Request text:** "Snorlax usually seems to laze around, but you'd be amazed how it can move when it runs!"
- **Solution:**
  - Get a picture of Snorlax running by waking it up with Fluffruit and Illumina Orbs in the path, then in the flowerfields, throw Illumina Orbs at it and photograph it running to you
  - On Secret Side Path (Day) level 1, take the alternate route through Snorlax's lair. Toss four Fluffruit into its mouth, waiting for each to be eaten. Immediately after the fourth, play melody. Snorlax gets up and watches you leave. Continue to the flower field, orb the walking Snorlax and quickly photograph its dash toward you. It stops before hitting you.
