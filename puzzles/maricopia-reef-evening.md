## Course: Maricopia Reef: Reef (Evening)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Maricopia Reef's evening course becomes available after completing the game.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 50,000, MAX = 90,000
- **Alternate routes:** Maricopia Reef (Evening) has one selectable detour and two automatic paths around Wailord. Face Wailord's tailfin to pass on its left, or face the second Wailord on the right to pass that way. Near the end, wait for the scan prompt, look slightly right and scan. Select the route icon for the brief detour.
