## Course: Fireflow Volcano: Volcano

- **How the course unlocks:** The volcano portion of the Fireflow Volcano unlocks after reaching the second research level in both the Sweltering Sands' nighttime course and the Lental Seafloor's Undersea.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 30,000, Lv. 3 = 60,000, MAX = 70,000
- **Alternate routes:** On Fireflow Volcano level 3, freely select the single alternate route. After entering the volcano, scan the right-hand passage and press X. It leads into a blue-magma cavern, then a burrow containing ancient ruins.
- *(source: https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/lental-region/fireflow-volcano-volcano · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*
