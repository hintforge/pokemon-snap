# Puzzles -- Index (LenTalk requests & hidden behaviors)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-10-11

New Pokémon Snap's puzzle layer is **LenTalk requests** -- tasks that point at a rare photo opportunity -- and the **hidden behavior triggers** behind 3- and 4-star photos (tool use, timing, chain reactions between Pokémon, research-level gating).

One file per course (`puzzles/<course-id>.md`). Each file opens with how the course unlocks, the research points each level needs, and its alternate routes, then lists every request with: the research level it needs, the exact posting trigger where a source states one, who asks, the reward, which Pokémon must be in the shot, the request's own text (the smallest nudge), and the full solution. Solutions are written out in full; the reader releases them according to your puzzle tier and hint-ladder requests.

Research points do not carry over: after each level-up the counter starts again from zero, which is why a MAX figure can be smaller than the Lv. 3 figure.

Course ids match `nav/architecture.md`.
