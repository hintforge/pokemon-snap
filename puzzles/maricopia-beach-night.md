## Course: Blushing Beach: Beach (Night)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Blushing Beach nighttime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Shiver Snowfields' nighttime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 50,000, MAX = 110,000
- **Alternate routes:** Blushing Beach (Night) has one shortcut. Scan beyond the rocky arch to reach a small hidden beach and skip the large rock formation on the main path.
