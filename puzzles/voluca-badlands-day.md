- **How the course unlocks:** The Barren Badlands' daytime course becomes available after completing the game.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 40,000, Lv. 3 = 65,000, MAX = 60,000
- **Alternate routes:** On Barren Badlands (Day) level 2 or 3, before the geyser, orb the Crystabloom beside the large round boulder. You slow down and the boulder falls. Scan and select the alternate route.

### A Brave Leap

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 and above of this course.
- **From:** Rita · **Reward:** Accessory 13 (Sticker)
- **Must be in the photo:** Tepig
- **Request text:** "I thought this might be dangerous, but then I realized that Tepic can probably make the jump! You should cheer it on, too!"
- **Solution:**
  - On the alternate path, scan nervous Tepig at the cliff ledge so it jumps down and approaches a larger cliff. Near the end, scan again. Photograph its running jump between the cliffs.

### Let's Play!

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 3 of this course.
- **From:** Rita · **Reward:** Design 22 (Frame)
- **Must be in the photo:** Diglett
- **Request text:** "Diglett doesn't seem interested in eating my fluffruit. Maybe it's not hungry... But we can toss a few more to it, just in case!"
