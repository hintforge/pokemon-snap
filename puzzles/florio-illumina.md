### Meganium's Pal

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 and above of this course. Posts when: Unlocked after clearing first Illumina Spot and reaching Florio Nature Park (Illumina Spot) Lv.2
- **From:** Rita · **Reward:** none listed
- **Must be in the photo:** Hoothoot, Meganium
- **Request text:** "When Meganium gets to this spot, it starts looking around kinda restlessly. Do you think it's looking for one of its friends?"
- **Solution:**
  - Get a picture of Meganium and Hoothoot by knocking Hoothoot out the tree right as Meganium gets to that point when running, and getting stunned
  - Step by step: (1) At Florio Nature Park (Illumina Spot), find sleeping Meganium ahead. → (2) Once it wakes, orb the cliff above the first Crystabloom to reveal Shaymin. → (3) Shaymin jumps onto Meganium's head as it passes. → (4) Orb the second Crystabloom just before they reach it. → (5) Meganium smells the flower and approaches the tree. → (6) Hit its pink leaves with Fluffruits when Meganium is near, bringing down Hoothoot. → (7) Photograph their greeting.
