## Course: Sweltering Sands: Sands (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Sweltering Sands daytime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Founja Jungle's nighttime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 30,000, Lv. 3 = 50,000, MAX = 60,000
- **Alternate routes:** Sweltering Sands (Day) has one alternate route with no prerequisites or level restriction. Descend the dune toward the oasis, scan and take the right branch.

### A Finicky Flame

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 1 and above of this course.
- **From:** Phil · **Reward:** none listed

### The Flying Cacnea

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 of this course. Posts when: Unlocked after reaching Sweltering Sands (Day) Lv.2

### Striking a Pose with its Tail

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 and above of this course. Posts when: Unlocked after reaching Sweltering Sands (Day) Lv.2
- **From:** Todd · **Reward:** Todd's Bag (Profile Image)
- **Must be in the photo:** Skorupi
- **Request text:** "Skorupi has such a unique tail. See if you can get a photo of it striking an interesting pose with its tail!"
- **Solution:**
  - Get a picture of Skorupi posing with its tail by using Illumina Orbs on the Crystablooms and then another at the Skorupi
  - Step by step: (1) Near the start, find the small hurricane on the right. → (2) Orb all three nearby Crystablooms to calm it and reveal Skorupi in the sand. → (3) Mandibuzz drops another Skorupi beside it. Orb either Skorupi so both raise their tails in warning. → (4) Photograph a raised tail.
- *(sources: https://www.serebii.net/newpokemonsnap/missions/strikingaposewithitstail.shtml ; https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/requests/striking-a-pose-with-ist-tail ; https://samurai-gamers.com/new-pokemon-snap/striking-a-pose-with-its-tail-request-walkthrough/ · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*
