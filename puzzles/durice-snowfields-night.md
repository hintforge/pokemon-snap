## Course: Shiver Snowfields: Snowfields (Night)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Shiver Snowfields' nighttime course unlocks after completing the Shiver Snowfields' daytime course at least once.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 45,000, MAX = 115,000
- **Alternate routes:** Shiver Snowfields (Night) has two alternate routes. First unlock the pine ravine by day: photograph Alolan Sandslash on the large left snow pile near the start. It burrows away and reappears further ahead on another left pile. Photograph it again. Near the snowy plateau incline, let it dig into the snowbank, then scan for the ravine. This also opens the night route. At night, take it into the pine forest. Orb Crabominable on the right, then again near a pine tree. Its punch drops snow onto sleeping Abomasnow, which wakes and creates a blizzard revealing Froslass. Photograph Froslass to open the Icy Cave route for both night and day.
