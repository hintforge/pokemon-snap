## Course: Maricopia Reef: Reef (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Maricopia Reef daytime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Blushing Beach's daytime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 35,000, Lv. 3 = 45,000, MAX = 60,000
- **Alternate routes:** Maricopia Reef (Day) has one selectable detour and two automatic paths around surfacing Wailord. Face Wailord's tailfin to pass on its left, or face the second Wailord on the right to pass that way. Near the course end, wait for the scan prompt, turn slightly right and scan. Select the route icon for the brief detour.
