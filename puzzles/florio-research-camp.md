## Course: Research Camp

- **How the course unlocks:** The Research Camp becomes available after completing the Fireflow Volcano's Illumina Spot at least once.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): MAX = 150,000
- **Alternate routes:** Research Camp has two starting points, determined by the main-menu view: Professor Mirror's laboratory starts you near the NEO-ONE vessels; the lit campfire starts you at the entrance. Retrying after photo evaluation keeps the same route. The only detour goes through the laboratory. Scan beside its door steps and hold X to enter. You return to those steps afterward.
- *(source: https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/lental-region/research-camp · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*
