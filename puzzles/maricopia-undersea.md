## Course: Lental Seafloor: Undersea

- **How the course unlocks:** After reaching the second research level of the Maricopia Reef's daytime course, the Lental Seafloor's Undersea unlocks.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 35,000, Lv. 3 = 75,000, MAX = 90,000
- **Alternate routes:** Undersea has two alternate routes. On level 2, follow Clawitzer toward the kelp area. At the wall recess, orb it just before it fires at the boulder blocking a tunnel. The stronger blast destroys the boulder, opening the caves and depths. On level 3, the entrance is already clear. Take this first route to reach the second. While descending, orb Lanturn. Past the rising Wailord, orb Lanturn again to free it from two Frillish. At the seafloor, follow Lanturn to a wall crack. Scan and confirm with X to enter the second route.
- *(source: https://www.grindosaur.com/en/games/new-pokemon-snap/lental-region/lental-seafloor-undersea · capture: directed pull 2026-10-11 · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 5 · puzzle-tier: 3)*
