### The Power of Xerneas

- **Unlock / level:** Research Level 2 and above of this course.
- **From:** Professor Mirror · **Reward:** Professor Mirror (Surprised) (Profile Image)
- **Must be in the photo:** Xerneas
- **Request text:** "The Illumina energy in this area seems particularly strong. We may be able to observe Xerneas behaving in new ways here."
- **Solution:**
  - Get a picture of Xerneas using Geomancy in the ruins
  - At Ruins of Remembrance Illumina Spot, research level 2+, enter the second room. Orb the light above the pond beneath the collapsed ceiling: it lands on a rocky platform as Xerneas. After it jumps across the water, orb it again. Quickly wake both Houndoom with Fluffruit, then scan to draw them toward Xerneas. They approach howling. Photograph Xerneas on its hind legs emitting rainbow rays.
