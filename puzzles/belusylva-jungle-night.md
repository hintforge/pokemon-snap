## Course: Founja Jungle: Jungle (Night)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Founja Jungle nighttime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Founja Jungle's daytime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 45,000, MAX = 115,000
- **Alternate routes:** Founja Jungle (Night) has three alternate routes. Near Quagsire's pond, orb the Crystabloom before the tall grass, scan the glowing paw prints and hold X. This also unlocks the daytime route. For the night-only ruins route, skip the left turn at the pond. In the right clearing, look up and right; orb sleeping Liepard on the small cliff, then hit it with Fluffruit. It takes time to respond. Continue to the sloping tree; the Liepard follows. Orb another Liepard high on a large branch. It jumps onto the NEO-ONE, knocking you off the trunk onto the route near the ruins. For the waterfall route, play melody to wake Liepard on the stone pillar. After it stretches and leaves, scan to go behind the waterfalls.
