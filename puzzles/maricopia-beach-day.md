## Course: Blushing Beach: Beach (Day)

- **How the course unlocks:** The Blushing Beach daytime course unlocks after reaching the second research level of the Founja Jungle's nighttime course.
- **Research points to reach each level** (the counter restarts at zero after each level-up): Lv. 2 = 30,000, Lv. 3 = 50,000, MAX = 70,000
- **Alternate routes:** Blushing Beach (Day) has one shortcut. Past the rocky arch, scan to enter a small hidden beach, bypassing the main route through the large rock formation.
